## Запрос для ImageGen

> Create one square edge-to-edge photographic contact sheet to be used as a single CSS sprite asset for a Russian dark focus / ambient audio website. Exactly 3 columns and 3 rows, nine equally-sized perfectly square images aligned to exact thirds of canvas. The panels must meet flush edge to edge; zero gutters, frames, dividers, gradients between images, captions, numbers, text, logos or browser artwork. Every cell is a clearly different cinematic nature photo or photoreal astronomical scene, with one recognizable subject near the middle so it works as a small square tile and as a large clipped panel. Overall quiet nighttime dark aesthetic, contrast within each scene, sharp fine detail and no overly saturated neon. Top row left to right: (1) raindrops streaking a dark window with falling rain beyond it, soft blue forest bokeh, (2) quiet misty hills reflected in a still lake at blue hour, warm soft amber horizon, (3) luminous soft violet and cyan emission nebula with a star field. Middle row left to right: (4) blue crescent planet viewed from orbit, cold black space and tiny stars, (5) one songbird perched on a leafy branch at dawn, green forest with soft amber light, (6) rolling ocean wave and white foam with dark turquoise water in soft dusk light. Bottom row left to right: (7) tall silver-green grasses visibly bending in a wind gust across a dusky meadow, (8) branching lightning beyond heavy storm clouds above a distant dark ridge, (9) a single small cricket perched on a blade of grass in a quiet dark meadow, clear brown cricket silhouette, muted gold and indigo background. Strong direct subject for every tile, no repeated compositions, no UI, text-free.
